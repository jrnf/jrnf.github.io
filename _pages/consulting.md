I work with universities, nonprofits, foundations, and companies on questions at the intersection of computing, education, and workforce. My perspective combines a background in computer science and machine learning, two decades on the faculty at Duke, six years leading education and workforce investments at the National Science Foundation, and current national leadership work at the Computing Research Association.

## Areas of work

**Program strategy and design.** Designing, scaling, and evaluating computer science and AI education and workforce programs, including efforts to broaden participation in computing.

**Funding landscape and proposal strategy.** Navigating federal and philanthropic funding, understanding how programs are structured and reviewed, and shaping competitive proposals and partnerships.

**AI education.** Helping institutions and organizations think through what AI means for curricula, faculty development, and student preparation.

**Talks, panels, and workshops.** Keynotes and facilitated sessions on the future of computing education and the computing workforce.

## Working together

Engagements vary in size, from a single conversation to a longer advisory relationship. To discuss a possible collaboration, please email me at [jforbes@stanfordalumni.org](mailto:jforbes@stanfordalumni.org).
