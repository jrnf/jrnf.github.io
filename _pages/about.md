I am Director of Strategic Initiatives and Partnerships at the [Computing Research Association](https://cra.org) (CRA). I work with CRA's Committee on Widening Participation and CRA's artificial intelligence education projects to improve and broaden access to computer science and artificial intelligence education.

Before joining CRA, I served from 2019 to 2025 as lead Program Director for the Education & Workforce program in the U.S. National Science Foundation's Directorate for Computer & Information Science & Engineering. In that role I led federal efforts to support communities of educators and researchers developing innovative approaches to build a robust workforce well prepared for careers in tech.

From 2001 to 2019 I was on the faculty at Duke University, most recently as Associate Professor of the Practice of Computer Science. I received my BS in computer science from Stanford University and my PhD in computer science from the University of California, Berkeley, where my dissertation, advised by Stuart Russell, applied reinforcement learning to autonomous vehicles. My early research was in machine learning and AI, and my work since has centered on computing education and broadening participation.
