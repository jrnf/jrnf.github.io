I work with CRA's Committee on Widening Participation and CRA's artificial intelligence education projects to improve and broaden access to computer science and artificial intelligence education. Before joining CRA, I served from 2019 to 2025 as lead Program Director for the Education & Workforce program in the U.S. National Science Foundation's Directorate for Computer & Information Science & Engineering. In that role, I led federal efforts to support the educators and researchers developing innovative approaches to prepare a strong, diverse tech workforce. My perspective combines research, education, and policy. I began in machine learning and AI, earning my PhD at UC Berkeley under Stuart Russell with a dissertation applying reinforcement learning to autonomous vehicles, after a BS in computer science from Stanford. I then spent nearly two decades at Duke University (2001-2019) completing my teaching experience as an Associate Professor of the Practice of Computer Science working directly with students while shifting my research toward computing education and broadening participation.
